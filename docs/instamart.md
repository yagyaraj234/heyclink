## What another chatbot already gets

Swiggy offers an Instamart connection for ChatGPT and other MCP clients. Once a user signs in, a supported assistant can select their address, search available pack sizes, build a cart, request checkout and track delivery. Its 16 published tools also expose past orders and frequent or recent purchases at an address. This gives another assistant evidence for what “wahi wala atta” might mean. [Consumer guide](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/start/consumer/) · [Instamart reference](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/) · [Frequent purchases](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/your_go_to_items/)

Swiggy and Sarvam demonstrated ordering Instamart groceries by phone and announced conversational commerce on Indus, Sarvam’s chat app. Multilingual voice ordering is already a competitive baseline. [Swiggy announcement, 8 April 2026](https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/press-release/swiggy-partners-with-sarvam-to-bring-indias-first-multilingual-voice-led-commerce-to-food-delivery-instamart-and-dineout/)

The opportunity to test is the handoff between two people. A cook can request groceries and a salary advance in one note. Our action planner can draft the cart, flag uncertain items and address a separate UPI payment link to the cook. The published Instamart tools cover payments to buy groceries. They offer no incoming WhatsApp voice-note channel or salary-advance workflow. Adding MCP alone leaves the forwarding step in place. ## What a live integration needs

1. **User session.** During the original 15-minute spike, the production endpoint at `https://mcp.swiggy.com/im` returned HTTP 401 with OAuth metadata. Dynamic client registration returned 201. A localhost redirect reached Swiggy's phone/OTP screen. Authenticated tool execution remains unverified. Swiggy ties account operations to the signed-in user. [Consumer guide](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/start/consumer/)
2. **Builder access.** Swiggy reviews custom-client applications, including redirects, use case, traffic estimate and demo video. Local prototyping can use a stub. An allowlisted redirect unlocks staging with seeded data. Production follows at least 48 hours of green staging, beyond this project's freeze. [Onboarding requirements](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/operate/access/)
3. **Preserve the existing cart.** Search at the selected address for available variants. Use the returned SKU and spin identifiers and pack counts. Because `update_cart` replaces the complete cart, read the user's existing cart and include the lines they want to keep. Surface stock removals and quantity caps from the response. [Cart contract](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/update_cart/)
4. **Confirm the actual purchase.** Show the updated bill, delivery address and selected payment method, then obtain explicit confirmation before checkout even when the total falls under the household limit. Multi-store checkout can succeed for some stores only. [Checkout contract](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/checkout/)
5. **Read payment availability for each cart.** Swiggy's GitHub manifest describes COD only. Its checkout reference also describes UPI and Swiggy Money. Query `get_payment_options` before offering a method. For a pending UPI payment, check status, then confirm the order after success. None of these methods was exercised in this spike. [Manifest](https://github.com/Swiggy/swiggy-mcp-server-manifest#instamart) · [Payment options](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/get_payment_options/) · [Checkout contract](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/reference/instamart/checkout/)
